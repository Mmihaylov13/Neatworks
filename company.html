About Neatworks

Design, manufacturing and installation of bespoke contemporary furniture — tailored to you, whether at home or on site.

Neatworks Carpentry Ltd specializes in design, manufacturing and installation of bespoke contemporary fitted and free standing furniture to the customer's personal requirements. We work with the general public as well as contract work from small and medium sized building companies and the education sector.

We are young company with solid background in the industry, willing to run the extra mile to provide superb service at reasonable cost. Our partners are local, reliable businesses, so you do not have to worry about delays or long waiting times.

We only use the best quality materials, fixings and fittings, to ensure perfect finish, extended life and enjoyable use. We are also very conscious about the environment — most of our wood based panels come from FSC and PEFC certified companies. All rubbish created during our work is recycled if it can not be reused, down to the packaging of the smallest elements.

Why us

We work with the best products on the market. We will tailor our services around your lifestyle to deliver the product you desire. Most of our work is done off site so minimum disruption of your life and environment is caused.

Our approach

  1. Normally an initial visit is agreed at suitable for you time, where you would lay down your idea and the options will be discussed.
  2. Then a time frame and way of communication will be set for quotation to be forwarded to you. A further visit could be scheduled should you feel that such is needed to clear every little detail.
  3. Finally we get to work off site and prepare all source materials. Then we come to your home / office and assemble the final product.
The process is as easy as 1-2-3.
